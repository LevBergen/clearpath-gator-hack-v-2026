# Clearpath: the pitch and likely questions

## A 30-second explanation

“One application question can ask for four things at once. A well-written paragraph can still leave two out. Clearpath keeps the original question visible, proposes its parts, and asks for missing details. You confirm your facts and review the answer before downloading. Our example is a student whose broken laptop is affecting class: she explained the problem, but still needs to give an amount and say what other help she tried.”

## What makes this different from asking a chatbot?

A chatbot can also help draft or check an answer. Clearpath puts that task into a repeatable workflow: original question, suggested parts, supporting excerpts, missing details, explicit confirmations, editable answer, final review. Changing the answer invalidates old checks; restoring work resets them. The contribution is the workflow and its state handling. Do not claim that form filling or question analysis is a new invention.

## Where is AI doing useful work?

Gemma suggests a question breakdown and details drawn from the supplied notes. The code checks exact quoted spans, validates boundaries, and manages confirmations. Joining confirmed details into the final answer is deterministic. The model cannot mark a human review complete or submit the form.

## What happens if the model is wrong?

The original wording stays visible. The applicant can edit details, remove mistaken parts, or discard the breakdown and answer directly. Source matching checks that quoted text exists, not that the model understood it. There is no guarantee that all requirements were found.

## Does this improve applications in practice?

We have a working prototype, synthetic examples, live model exercises, and automated regression checks. We have not run an applicant study or measured acceptance rates, time saved, or real-world error reduction. Testing with applicants and support staff is the next step.

## Why choose this rather than an investing or trend-based project?

The problem has a specific user, an understandable failure, and an outcome judges can see in a short demo. An investing tool would need a similarly specific advantage plus credible data and evaluation. The subject alone would not establish novelty. For this entry, demonstrate a narrow task working well.

## Where does the information go?

The app has no application database or automatic browser persistence. Choosing AI drafting sends the form and notes to Google. Saving and exporting are explicit local downloads. The browser/OS provides read-aloud. Use fictional data in judging.

## What is unfinished?

Text input only. No PDF/image parsing, external website filling, funding discovery, fact verification, eligibility decisions, or actual submission. Local Ollama has an adapter but has not been exercised on this machine.

## If the internet or model is unavailable

Open the clearly labeled illustrative challenge, confirm the two proposed details, enter the amount and other-help answer, build, review, and download. Explain that this is the offline interaction path, not fresh model inference. Keep the submitted recording available as a backup.
